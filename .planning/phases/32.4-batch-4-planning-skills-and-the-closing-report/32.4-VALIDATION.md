## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** run the touched module (`python3 -m unittest tests.<module>`); the `staged-tests` hook also runs the covering modules.
- **After every plan wave:** `make test-unit` (plus `make e2e-test` after the curated-config plan).
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** `make test`, `make check-dogfood` and `python3 tools/judge_loop.py check .planning/judge` exit 0; the custom-tmpdir row of `make test-env-matrix` once after the env-matrix change.
- **Max feedback latency:** about 60 seconds per task.
