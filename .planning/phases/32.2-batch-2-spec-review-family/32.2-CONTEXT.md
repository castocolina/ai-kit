### Cheap-execution ranking (SPEC R4, HUB-04)

- **D-01: The floor applies per call, not per entry.** In `score_candidates`, a floor axis counts only when at least
  one entry of that call carries a finite value for it; when none does, that axis's floor is skipped for the call.
  This covers the fully null catalog and the no-key case (no `scores` at all). In a mixed catalog the floor works as
  today: an entry with a missing, non-numeric or below-35 coding index is excluded, so models of unknown strength never
  outrank real scores (the 49.0 missing-axis rule, `model_ranker.py:38-48`). `test_missing_or_non_numeric_floor_axis_is_excluded`
  and `test_floor_boundary_is_inclusive` (`tests/test_ai_kit_spec.py:6687-6719`) stay green unchanged.
  - choice 0.96 (typesafe: skip_when_none_has_it; laya low 0.39 fallback_score).
- **D-02: The change lives in `model_ranker.py` only.** `score_candidates` narrows the floor before `_passes_floor`;
  its docstring and the comment at `:38-48` describe the per-call rule. `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS` and the toml values stay
  (floor `{"coding_index": 35}`, pinned by `test_bundled_mechanical_floor_is_coding_35_only` and the mirror test);
  the `[mechanical.floor]` comment in `references/ranking-weights.toml:57-60`, which says the ranking is empty without
  a key, is rewritten to the per-call rule. Analyzer, Confident; not asked.
- **D-03: curated-config's golden tests are rerun, never regenerated silently.** The golden catalog
  (`tests/fixtures/curated_config_v2/model-catalog.json`) carries real coding indexes, so the floor should behave as
  today; the plan runs `tests.test_curated_config_golden` after the edit, and any golden change is traced to a
  per-runtime pool with no coding value and explained in the plan summary. choice 0.94 (typesafe: rerun_goldens;
  laya low 0.53 add_pinned_test).
- **D-04: Fixture and tests.** `tests/fixtures/model_catalog_null_index.json`: a `_meta` key (the test skips it, as
  AGENTS.md requires of catalog readers) and synthetic `example/<model>` entries with round-number prices, speeds
