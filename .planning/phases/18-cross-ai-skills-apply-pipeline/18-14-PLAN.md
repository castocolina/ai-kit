Design notes:
- plan-set result: `op` `set`, `source` `user`, why `set by the user (plan-set)`, `status` and
  `candidates` removed; equal to `existing` -> `op` `keep` with `source` `user`.
- `--force` overrides only the candidate-style refusals: a non-candidate value for a
  `needs_choice` entry, a `review.default_reviewers` slug outside the inventory lanes, and the
  D-44 command guard. These refuse even with `--force`: a key the probe rejected, a
  `workflow.cross_ai_timeout` below 1800 or not an integer, a string the strict encoder refuses,
  a skill that is not installed or not addressable, and a trusted root that is not
  `~/`-relative. D-17 names only the timeout (and 18-15's `plan-unset` of an absent key); the
  other four are a recorded deviation (ledger, Round 2) that 18-22 writes into 18-CONTEXT.
