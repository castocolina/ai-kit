# Phase 38.1: Runner error classification and retry policy — Specification

**Created:** 2026-11-16
**Ambiguity score:** 0.15 (gate: ≤ 0.20)
**Requirements:** 10 locked *(amended 2026-11-16, free-lane pool study, 38.1 D-23 to D-27: 11 locked; R11 added for the blocked-until record of a usage-limited lane, PROVISIONAL; R1 to R10 unchanged)*

## Goal

Every failure the runner (`cross-ai.mjs`) sees gets a class from the channel the host actually used, and the retry policy the
owner chose (2026-11-16, recorded here) sets the runner's defaults. The class list becomes one closed list with a locking
test, apart from Phase 37's setting reasons and Phase 38's outcomes `partial` and `locked`. The catalogue covers the real-run
bodies it misses today and every host's own "model does not exist" wording captured live; a plain-text limit on stdout gets the
same class as one on stderr; a router cooldown line is a quota cycle with its reset time on a confirmed router lane; and the
hard tier becomes a short first check, then a check every 10 minutes until the 4-hour cap, plus one extra check just after a
reset time the body states.

## Background

(`.planning/milestones/v1.3-OWNER-FOLLOWUP.md`, sections 1 and 3) and the owner's recorded retry rule (todo 2026-10-14: "re-check
every 10 minutes, capped at 4 hours"). Phase 38 (`quota_cycle` is a Python dispatch class, `partial` and `locked` are runner
outcomes, not host errors) and Phase 37 (the setting-reason list, `--describe`, the status line group) are inputs.

**The owner's decisions (highest priority):**

- 2026-11-16, answers to this phase's question batch: (1) the hard tier is a short first check, then every 10 minutes up to the
  4-hour cap; waits under 60 s that the caller cannot schedule run inside the call when the time limit allows; (2) a stated
  reset time adds one extra check just after it, only when it lies inside the cap, the 10-minute checks continue so a wrong
  stated time never blocks a lane, and a time beyond the cap changes nothing; (3) a direct lane keeps the 2026-11-02 rule (a
  quota error stops at once; only confirmed router lanes wait), and the requirement text "periodic retries for quota errors"
  means router lanes only.
- 2026-11-02 (kept as is): auth stops without retry; off-router quota stops; router-tail credential rows count as quota on a
  confirmed router lane; the runner never switches harness, provider or model.
- A table is seed data, never a decider (2026-11-16 directive). The catalogue is such a table: each row carries its label and
  evidence, a row never blocks a lane, and live evidence beats a row. Only `router-env/<x>` model names may be hardcoded; every
  fixture in this phase uses placeholders.
- jev and herdr only when available at the caller's runtime, never in the runner, every path with a tested no-jev, no-herdr
  behavior.

- The shipped retry is not a single wait (the RUN-11 and roadmap wording is stale, written against Phase 25). Defaults: five
  same-session retries waiting 1, 1.5, 2.25, 3.4, 5.1 s (about 13 s), one session reset, then the hard tier: cycle n exits 75 with
  `retry_after_s=hard_waits_s[n]` (600, 900, 1800), and the re-invoke after the last entry exits 69 `stopped=hard-waits`; the

  stops after 55 minutes and the cap never binds. `hard_waits_s` takes 1 to 6 entries, so the owner's rule (24 checks of 10
  minutes) cannot be written as a list. A quota class stops at once off-router and cycles on a confirmed router lane; transient
  cycles on every lane.
