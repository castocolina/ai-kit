# Runner retry policy — owner decision record (Phase 38.1, RUN-11)

**Decided:** 2026-11-16 by the owner, answering the three questions of this phase's batch. Written by the phase subagent.
**Applies to:** `skills/ai-kit-spec-review/references/cross-ai.mjs` (the retry cascade), ai-kit's `config.toml` `[runner.retry]`,
the rendered `CONFIG.retry` and `RETRY_DEFAULTS`. Spec: `38.1-SPEC.md` R5.

## Decision

1. **Hard tier.** After the soft series (five same-session retries over about 13 s, one session reset, unchanged), the caller
   waits a short first check (60 s), then 300 s, then every 600 s until the 4-hour episode cap. Keys: `hard_waits_s` default
   `[60, 300]`, new `hard_every_s` default 600 (0 restores the list-end stop); the cap is `capS` 14400 and cannot be raised. A
   wait under 60 s (a configured value) that the caller cannot schedule runs inside the call when the remaining deadline
   admits it, else it becomes a 60 s hint (plan detail: the admission test is the soft step's, with the 60 s reserve).
2. **A stated reset time** (the allowlisted fields and the router's `(reset after ...)` display field) adds one extra check at the
   stated time plus 120 s, only when that is at least 60 s ahead, inside the cap's remaining time and earlier than the next
   periodic check. The periodic checks continue, so a wrong stated time never blocks a lane; a time beyond the cap, in the past
   or unparseable changes nothing; each stated time is used once per episode. It never lengthens a wait or lifts a stop.
3. **Which lanes wait.** 5xx and other transient faults on every lane; quota only on a confirmed router lane. A direct lane keeps
   the 2026-11-02 rule (a quota error stops at once). Auth, billing, permission, bad model and bad request stop. The wording
   "periodic retries for quota errors" in RUN-11 is read as router lanes only.

## Evidence (live, 2026-11-16)

- Shipped before this decision: soft 1, 1.5, 2.25, 3.4, 5.1 s, one session reset, then 600, 900, 1800 s and a stop
  (`stopped=hard-waits`) after 55 minutes; the cap never bound; `hard_waits_s` held at most 6 entries. The owner's recorded rule
  (todo 2026-10-14) was "re-check every 10 minutes, capped at 4 hours".
- Router cooldown episodes in the router container's own log (34, six models, 30 days): 24 under 2 minutes, 1 of 2 to 10 minutes,
  1 of 10 to 60 minutes, none of 1 to 4 hours, 8 over 4 hours (up to 24 hours); p10 11 s, p50 60 s, p75 3,465 s, p90 16,478 s.
- Real runner status lines in past sessions since 2026-10-24: 1,190 lines; 84 retry episodes: 35 ended ok (most after the first
  600 s wait), 2 reached the end of the ladder, 8 ended in another failure, 39 have no later line. Hard-tier hints seen: 600 s
  (about 66), 900 s (4), 1800 s (1), one 20 s and one 63 s deadline-clamped. Too few episodes reached 900 s or 1800 s to compare
  later waits; the logs do not time the recoveries.
- Recorded earlier: a quota window clears about 2 minutes after its stated reset (hence the 120 s margin); one stated reset 146
  hours away cleared in about 10 minutes (hence the periodic checks continue and a stated time never replaces them).
- Not verified: whether the router's cooldown wording reaches a client body (the runner's real cooldown body is
  `model <x> is unavailable`).

## Why this shape

24 of 34 cooldowns end within 2 minutes, so a first check at 600 s wastes about 9 minutes on most; 8 of 34 outlast the cap, so
a stated time beyond the cap is information that costs nothing to ignore and the periodic series is bounded by the cap anyway.
The owner's 10-minute rule is kept from the third check on.

## Consequences to watch

- An idle-killed attempt (Phase 38 D-04) is a timed-out transient attempt, so a lane that hangs every time now retries about
  every 10 minutes for up to 4 hours; `hard_every_s = 0` or a lower `max_retries` stops that sooner.
- The caller's one-shot wait rule still holds per hint (one wait per `retry_after_s`), but an episode can now hold about 24
  hints; `ai-kit-cross-ai` section 6 and the GSD retry, skip, abort answer text are updated by the plan.
