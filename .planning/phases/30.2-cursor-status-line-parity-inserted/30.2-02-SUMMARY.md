## What was built

- `ConfigSpec.host_scalars` (data only) and `Config.host_knobs`; `cfg_coerce` gains the `number` kind (finite, >= 0, bools refused); `cfg_scalar_kind`, `cfg_host_key_problem`, `cfg_bind_host_scalars`.
- `[cursor] extra_alert` (default true) and `[cursor] extra_alert_usd` (no default: no override). Env `AI_KIT_CURSOR_EXTRA_ALERT` and `AI_KIT_CURSOR_EXTRA_ALERT_USD` override TOML. A bad value keeps the default and is reported through both channels (TOML via `validate_config_file`, env via `_env_problems`).
- `cursor_quota.core_extra_alert(knobs, quota) -> ExtraAlert(fires, threshold, source)`: source `off`, `config`, `cursor` (fixed Cursor limit) or `none`; fires only for `config`/`cursor` at or above the threshold, at two decimals, with spend above zero.
- `--doctor --host cursor` prints, after the quota lines: the cycle line for the cache's period (monthly, weekly or unknown), `data difference: quota source (inferred — undocumented Cursor API); the line marks it with ≈`, one `unverified on Cursor:` line per key of `UNVERIFIED_CURSOR_SEGMENTS`, then `extra-spend alert: ...`. The cursor-limit and no-limit alert lines name a non-ok quota state.
- `QuotaResult.included_period` (carried only when in `PERIOD_ENUM`); `core_synthetic_cache` ends 17 days out.
