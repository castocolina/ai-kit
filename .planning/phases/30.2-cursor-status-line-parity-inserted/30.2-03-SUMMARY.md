## Accomplishments

- Cursor included and API gauges: the wide form is `⚡️ inc: <sign><pct>% (↺ <%b %d>, <N>d) ≈`, then `⚡️ inc: <sign><pct>% ≈`, then the unsigned narrow `⚡️ inc: <pct>% ≈`. At a fixed clock on the synthetic cache the joined line equals the owner's wide target byte for byte.
- `cursor_reset` renders `↺ <%b %d> (<N>d) ≈` (wide) or `↺ <%b %d> ≈` (narrow); the time of day is gone.
- Days left are `max(0, ceil((end - now) / 86400))`: 23 h gives `1d`, 25 h gives `2d`, a reset at the render moment gives `0d`, and a passed reset on a stale result gives `0d` with ` ≈ stale`.
- The extra-spend alert colours only the used amount with the theme's `RED+bold`; the sign, `/$<ceiling>`, the icon and the `≈` marker keep the gauge colour. The rule is `cursor_quota.core_extra_alert`, shared with the doctor.
- `seg_alt_time_ago` lives once in `common_segments` and runs on both hosts through `common_probe.probe_transcript_ago`; Claude's copy and `probe_ago` are removed.
- Claude's `probe_rss_bytes` is `probe_ancestor_rss_bytes(probe_is_claude, 8)`; the Claude RSS tests keep their expectations, re-patched to the walk's readers in `common_probe`.
- Icons: `📂` path (every tier), `✏️` git_dirty, `🤖` model, `⌛` alt_time_ago, `📐` alt_term_dimensions. `✏` joins `_ICON_VS16` and U+270F joins `_WIDE_BMP`.
- `📐 <cols>` when the row count is 0 or less (Cursor's `render_width_chars` 116 gives `📐 116` and no `×0`); `📐 <cols>×<lines>` otherwise, keeping the `?` mark.
- `· max` after the model name (after `param_summary`) when `ctx.model_max` is true.
