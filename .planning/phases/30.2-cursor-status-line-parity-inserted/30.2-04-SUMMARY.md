## Decisions Made

- **Sample-parity scope (R10, D-09), stated once:** the compared entries are the four Cursor-only entries (`cursor_included`, `cursor_api`, `cursor_extra`, `cursor_reset`) and every shared entry carrying `cursor_sample` (`model`). Shared entries without `cursor_sample` (`path`, `git_branch`, and so on) keep their host-neutral samples. The comparison strips ANSI and the leading icon with its VS16, masks the reset date (`[A-Z][a-z]{2} \d{2}`), the day count (`\d+d)`) and clock times, then compares byte for byte. A builder returning nothing fails the test.
- **Sample payload key set:** `CURSOR_PAYLOAD_FIELDS` is computed in the test as the dotted key paths of `payload_synthetic.json` and `payload_poc_redacted.json`; design §5.1 is cited only for the two Claude-only exclusions.
- **Icon parity seeding:** the Cursor context is built through `cursor_host.HOST.build_context` over the sample payload with the synthetic cache written at the fixed clock, then the probes that would otherwise touch git, `/proc` or the transcript are seeded (`git`, `ago`, `rss`, `t_start`, `slowest`) and the transcript path points at a scratch file.
- **Empty-cache negative:** the entry script runs with a scratch HOME and an empty `XDG_CACHE_HOME` (not `_environment`, which always writes the cache). No `inc:`, `api:` or `extra` text appears.
