## Accomplishments

- The canonical catalog is `cross-ai-errors.json` (73 rows). `python3 -m ai_kit_spec.cross_ai_catalog --write` splices it into the runner's `// <patterns>` block, and `--check` plus `tests/test_cross_ai_catalog.py` fail on any byte drift. `tools/gen_test_groups.py` registered the module in `Makefile` and `.pre-commit-config.yaml`.
- OmniRoute reason codes are tier-0 rows of their own, and every 25.1-EVIDENCE.md sample is an `evidence-*` fixture. A body matching a reason code and a provider row takes the reason code; a Kimi 403 is quota, a bare `4021 tokens` matches nothing, and the GLM 1310 body matches its structured code.
- Live classification walks the same tier-ordered table as post-hoc classification and arms a kill only when the winning row is terminal (and, for an event, not transient). `terminalTable` and `eventTable` are gone.
- The owner overlay at `${XDG_CONFIG_HOME:-$HOME/.config}/ai-kit/cross-ai-errors.json` passes the same `validateRow` checks as shipped rows. A bad row is refused by id. All overlay rows of one text run in one `node:vm` call under a 50 ms timeout, and 200 ms of measured vm time disables the overlay for the rest of the call.

## classifyStop callers

`classifyStop` now takes `route` and `model` and derives the router lane from `route === 'router'` when `routerLane` is not passed. Each caller:

- `classifyOne` passes `entry.route || 'unknown'` and `entry.model || ''`.
- `runClassifyOnly` passes the case route (`router`, `unknown`, `router-inferred`, or the route resolved from the model) and `entry.model || ''`.
- `runCli` passes the call's `opts.route || 'unknown'` and the call's model on both the timed-out and the finished post-hoc path.
- `liveMatch` passes the route and model it receives, and `runOnce` forwards the call's route and model into every `liveMatch` call (stderr line, event, partial buffer).
- `hitForLane` and the default table of `classifyStop` read `TABLE`. `main` sets `TABLE = mergeCatalog(PATTERNS, loadOverlay(process.env))` once per call, before `--classify-only` and before any run; `blackBoxSelfTest` resets `TABLE` to the shipped rows so pure cases start without the harness environment's overlay.

## Decisions Made

- `text-pre-dispatch` is removed. Its only evidence is the all-targets-skipped body, which `omniroute-all-targets-skipped` now classifies as `quota` on every route. On an unconfirmed route that is a one-attempt bare exit 75 (no `retry_after_s=`), where Phase 25's `permission_blocked` reading exited 69. 25.1-15 O7 states the change.
- Classes recorded for the owner, as the plan assumed: `no_executable_targets` and `model_not_in_catalog` are `bad_model`; the cooldown, circuit-open, endpoint-unavailable and model-unavailable codes are `transient`; `ALL_TARGETS_SKIPPED` is `quota` and not terminal-live.
- `--classify-only` accepts a fixture route of `router-inferred` in addition to `router` and `unknown`, so the member-402 inferred fixture can be classified without spawning. An unknown route string is still refused.
