<objective>

the soft series is spent or a soft step is refused for the deadline, the runner records the lane's

cap would pass, it exits 69 with `stopped=hard-waits` or `stopped=cap` and persists that stop on
every path, and a stopped record refuses further calls until it goes stale or the owner deletes it.
