<objective>

the soft series is spent, the runner records the lane's cycle and exits 75 with
`retry_after_s = hard_waits_s[cycle]`, and once the list is spent or the 4-hour cap would pass it
exits 69 with `stopped=hard-waits` or `stopped=cap` and persists that stop in the state file.

soft series into the `hard` step, deleted `cooldownS` and re-pinned every Phase 25 ladder case;
this plan adds only `hardWaitFor`, the cycle selection, the stop record and the route-keyed state
file, and re-pins only the cases whose output or setup those change.
