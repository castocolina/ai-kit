.planning/scripts/cross-ai.mjs --runtime opencode --model router-env/<referee> --route-opencode
router --mode review --outer-timeout 3600` under `node .claude/gsd-core/bin/gsd-tools.cjs
run-with-timeout 3600 --`, the prompt on stdin; the two referee lanes run serially per lane and
in parallel with each other and with the native subagents. Transient failures follow the runner's
ladder; an exit 75 with `retry_after_s=` is waited out once and the same lane re-dispatched; any
other stop goes to the owner verbatim and that lane's report says it did not finish. Never
switch a lane's harness, provider or model.
