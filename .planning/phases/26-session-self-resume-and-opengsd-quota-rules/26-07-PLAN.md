so the hook is the only actor that sees the reset text at the stall.

| T-26-23 | Elevation of privilege | hook arming unrelated sessions | medium | mitigate | `--only-if-armed`: the hook refreshes only a session its orchestrator armed |
| T-26-24 | Denial of service | hook stalls the host | low | mitigate | one 8 s monotonic deadline over the bounded stdin read and the `arm` child, 2 s under the row's 10 s; fail-open exit 0; `TestHookDeadline` |
