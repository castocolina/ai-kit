| **Estimated runtime** | subset seconds; `make test` about 7 min |

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the quick command for the touched modules.
- **After every plan wave:** `make test-unit` plus `make arch-test`.
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** `make test`, `make validate` and `make e2e-docker` green; skill-judge on every new or changed SKILL.md.
- **Max feedback latency:** about 60 s for a subset run.
