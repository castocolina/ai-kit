## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15.1-01 | Tampering | Chart labels and tooltips carrying row-derived family, subfamily and runtime strings | high | mitigate | Labels and tooltip content are handed to Chart.js through its own data API, which renders to canvas rather than to markup; the panel titles are fixed strings; no row-derived value is concatenated into HTML anywhere in this plan, and `test_no_data_driven_innerhtml_assignment` stays unmodified and is re-asserted by Task 1's verify gate |
