# Phase 29.1 Plan 03: Cascade, conflict resolution, hotfix and merge chain Summary

The cascade of a moved trunk or refolded lower unit runs in one leased atomic push, with a full restore on any refusal. A rebase conflict pauses, and `restack --continue` resumes it through the check, the gates and the publish. The hotfix branches from the target's remote tip outside the stack, and a merged hotfix cascades the open stack once. The owner-started merge chain merges bottom-up with merge commits, fast-forwards the local trunk, and cleans up checkout-safely. A gh-stack backend merges the ready prefix in one `gh stack merge` call. The gh-stack cascade is not built.
