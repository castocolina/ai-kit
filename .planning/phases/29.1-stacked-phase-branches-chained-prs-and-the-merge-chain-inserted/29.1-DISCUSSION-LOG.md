## Live facts that changed the plan

- gsd-core 1.18.0 dispatches no hook before `handle_branching`, and planning commits land on the checked-out
  branch: the branch must exist from the unit's first planning commit (D-01).
- `gh stack rebase` pauses on a conflict and `rebase --abort` restores every branch; plain `rebase --update-refs`
  rebases the whole stack and its abort restores every ref (D-07).
- Merge commits leave the upper PRs clean after a lower merge, so the merge chain never needs a restack (D-09).
- ai-kit has no CI workflows; the gh token lacks `delete_repo` (D-10, user-owned precondition).
- Local main is 14 planning commits ahead of `origin/main` (user-owned precondition).
