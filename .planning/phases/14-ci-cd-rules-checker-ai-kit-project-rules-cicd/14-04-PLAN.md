  without saying so.

  `SKILL.md`, so there is no baseline to regress against and none may be manufactured. Do not
  construct a synthetic baseline, do not reuse a sibling's score as a stand-in, and do not cite the
  design spec's historical figures for a different skill as if they applied here. The bar is zero
