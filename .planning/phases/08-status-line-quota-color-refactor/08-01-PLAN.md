## Formula Direction -- Resolved (was REVISION_CONFLICT)

Revision cycle 1's cross-AI review (`08-REVIEWS.md` cycle 1) flagged a genuine
mathematical conflict between ROADMAP SC1 (identical `used_percentage`, less
time remaining must render more urgently) and the then-locked burn-rate
formula `pct / elapsed_fraction` (CONTEXT.md's original D-01), which is
provably monotonically DECREASING in `elapsed_fraction` for fixed `pct` --
the opposite of SC1. This plan's prior revision correctly returned
`REVISION_CONFLICT` rather than silently picking a side (see `08-REVIEWS.md`'s
`## Plan-Revision Conflicts` entry for the full arithmetic proof and the
alternatives table).

**Resolution (2026-09-11):** switched the formula to
`ratio = used_percentage / remaining_fraction`, where
`remaining_fraction = 1 - elapsed_fraction`. For any fixed `pct`, this ratio
strictly INCREASES as `remaining_fraction` shrinks (i.e. as less time
remains), satisfying SC1 by construction. ROADMAP.md SC4's worked example and
CONTEXT.md D-01/D-03/D-04 were amended 2026-09-11 to match -- see ROADMAP.md's
own amendment note on SC4 and `08-CONTEXT.md`'s amended decisions. This
revision (cycle 2) implements the corrected formula throughout Task 1/Task 2,
with the division-by-zero risk now at `remaining_fraction == 0` (D-03, the
window's nominal reset moment -- clamp to max urgency) rather than at
`elapsed_fraction == 0` (now perfectly safe, reduces to raw `pct` with no
clamp), and D-04's past-reset fallback now clamping `remaining_fraction` to
`1.0` instead of `0.0`.

`must_haves.truths[0]` above reflects this resolution rather than flagging it
open.

<objective>
Replace raw-usage-percentage-only rate-limit bucket coloring with a burn-rate
signal (usage relative to remaining-fraction-of-window) that feeds the
EXISTING, unchanged `theme.ramps["rate"]` thresholds -- so two buckets with
identical `used_percentage` but different time remaining render different
urgency colors, while the displayed percentage and reset-suffix text stay
byte-identical.

Purpose: Today's status line colors a rate-limit bucket purely by how much
quota is used, ignoring how much of the window's time has already passed --
a user at 50% used with 4 hours left in a 5-hour window looks exactly as
urgent as a user at 50% used with 10 minutes left. This phase makes the
color reflect real urgency (ROADMAP Phase 8 goal).

Output: Two new pure `util_` functions in `tools/status-line.py`
(window-duration parsing + burn-rate ratio with the D-03/D-04 edge-case
clamps), one changed call site inside `util_rate_group_str`, and an extended
`tests/test_status_line.py` ramp-test suite covering the new formula's
boundaries at both the pure-function and rendered-segment level.
</objective>
