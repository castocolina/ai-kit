### IN-01: `PairRow` breaks the otherwise-alphabetical placement in `tool_catalog.py`'s `__all__`

**File:** `skills/_shared/ai_kit_rules_common/tool_catalog.py:740-756`
**Issue:** `__all__` is sorted per isort's constants-before-classes-before-functions convention (confirmed compliant with `RUF022`), which is why tooling accepts the current placement of `"PairRow"` after `"TOOL_CATEGORY_INDEX"`. A human skimming the list for "is X exported" may still find it slightly surprising that `PairRow` sits between the all-caps constants and the lowercase functions rather than near where a reader might first look (e.g., alphabetically before `REDUNDANT_VS_COMPLEMENTARY_PAIRS`). This is purely cosmetic — ruff's own sort-checker (and `--fix`) already consider the current ordering correct, so there is nothing to change unless the team wants a different convention.
**Fix:** No action required; noting only because a future contributor skimming by eye (rather than trusting the linter) could mistakenly "fix" the ordering and get flagged by CI. Leave as-is, or add a short comment noting the isort constants/classes/functions grouping if it comes up again.
