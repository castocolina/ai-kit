# Plan 21.1-05: Wizard replace gate Summary

**The wizard's housekeeping gate now offers backup-and-link for real content at skill paths — default No, explicit `y` only — with the PTY proof end-to-end.**

## Accomplishments
- `replace` is a third housekeeping kind (after `stale`, `predecessors`) listing real directories, files and foreign symlinks at claude/pi skill paths; default No — Enter and `n` leave everything alone (SPEC R8 honored by the Textual pilot and the PTY run).
- `apply_housekeeping(prune, repoint, replace=False)` runs 21.1-04's `backup_and_link` inside the stdout/stderr redirect; the captured `moved` log is appended to `log_sink` in `finally` and printed to stderr once the wizard exits — on confirm, abort and crash alike.
- Candidate sets for the three gates are computed once and are provably disjoint (unit-tested); a live foreign link only ever reaches the default-No `replace` gate.
- PTY e2e drives the real wizard through the gate under `uv run` with all five scratch host roots and asserts the backup only exists after `y`.
- README's installer section documents the gate ("default No") and its backup destination.
