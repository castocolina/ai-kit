  than a content edit. The pinned tokens (`cross-domain`, `fixed-order`, `entrypoint-resolution`,
  `report-only`) are hyphenated compounds a natural rephrasing would otherwise drop -- write the
  sentence around the token rather than adjusting the check to accept a different spelling.

  STOP -- do not apply it, and do not quietly record it as declined without saying so.

  Critical and zero Important findings, or a score of at least 96/120 (or the proportional equivalent

  search whose commands and output go into the evidence file.

  concision-versus-limits conflict is a STOP rather than a trade.** No limit statement may be dropped,
  merged, or reworded into something weaker to satisfy a concision-flavoured finding. If the gate
  cannot be cleared without doing so, STOP and report the tension with the gate uncleared -- that is
  the correct, complete outcome, not a failure to work around.
