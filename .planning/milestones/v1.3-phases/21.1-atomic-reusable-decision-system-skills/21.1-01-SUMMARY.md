## Accomplishments

- `skills/jev-fast-decision/scripts/jev_review.py` (1267 lines, executable, stdlib-only, `CLIENT_PROTOCOL = 2`): one `TABLE` of three rows carrying each backend's data (`remote`, `batch`, `min_confidence`, `models`, `model_env`, `response_model`, `retry_limit`, `state_chars_limit`) and its four adapters; selection, dispatch, probe and remote listing iterate `TABLE` and an AST test proves no backend-name literal escapes an adapter or the table.
- Laya dispatch is one request per item (the row's `batch` is false) and the whole D-13 taxonomy behaves as the 2026-10-11 todo says: 503 retried at most twice honoring `Retry-After`, an exhausted 503 escalating in auto (falling to the next usable row) while staying the item's error under `JEV_BACKEND=laya`; 500 / refused connection / timeout escalate without retry; 400/413/422 are caller errors.
- Size and trust guards: every body (200 and error) is read through `_read_body` capped at `_MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES` (1 MiB) before `json.loads` — an over-cap 200 fails only that request, its siblings keep their answers; `usage.truncated` or a state over `state_chars_limit` forces `needs_fallback` whatever `top` is; `_PROB_SUM_TOLERANCE = 2e-2` accepts 4-decimal Laya (0.9999) and 2-decimal API sums.
- Preflight, `JEV_MIN_CONFIDENCE`, the consent gate (`JEV_ALLOW_REMOTE`: `1` or a comma list; unknown names grant nothing) and the model-id pin per row (`JEV_LAYA_MODEL`, `JEV_TYPESAFE_MODEL`, `JEV_OPENCODE_MODEL`) all refuse before a socket opens; `--probe` never posts question content.
- `tests/test_jev_fast_decision.py`: 75 tests with a reusable module-level `JevStub` (in-process `ThreadingHTTPServer` on 127.0.0.1) that 21.1-03 can import; registered in the unit tier via `tools/gen_test_groups.py`; `TestFindClient` updated for D-02 (repo sibling wins, home path stays in `tried`).
- Evidence scaffold: `21.1-PRESSURE.md` (S1–S5 verbatim, the three arms, ten `pending (orchestrator)` RED rows, GREEN/REFACTOR placeholders) + `pressure/baseline-SKILL.md` (live copy, secret-scan clean, sha256 `9d606f45…`, no home path in either file).
